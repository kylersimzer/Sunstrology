      The sky from the Sun

      Sunstrology computes the solar system at any moment between 1800 and 2050 as seen from the Sun, then treats Earth as you. Your sign is Earth’s station: one of twelve 30° sectors of its orbit, named for the Sun’s own anatomy, from Core to Heliopause.

      The astronomy is real orbital mechanics (JPL Keplerian elements, solved with Kepler’s equation; the Moon from Meeus). The stations, patterns and readings are an invented symbolic layer, and the app keeps the two apart.

      No retrogrades, no houses, no borrowed zodiac. From the Sun, nothing ever moves backward.
