Fifty marks for a sun-centred almanac

Each mark is drawn on the same 24 × 24 grid as the stems.fm and GODMIX marks, in one white ink with round caps. The all-white mark gets locked in first. The banners and animated loops are then built from the one you pick. Star the ones you like, open any mark to see it large, at small sizes and as an app icon, then send the numbers back. Round 2 (26–50) pushes into the Sun’s anatomy, letterforms, the app’s own views and bolder filled silhouettes.
